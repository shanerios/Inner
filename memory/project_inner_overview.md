Inner is a React Native (Expo) audio-engineered experience app focused on:
- Lucid dreaming and OBE (out-of-body experience) induction
- Dream logging
- Meditation and relaxation

Key tech: RevenueCat (subscriptions/paywall), PostHog (analytics), Expo Updates (OTA).

Brand terms: "Sanctum Sphere", "Inner Resonance" — intentional, do not flag as repetition.

Planned future feature: AI Guide — not just an information index, but a personalized guide to help users with their lucid dreaming/OBE/meditation journey, also serving as a library for in-app lessons.

**Why:** Enhance the guided experience beyond static audio/lessons; make the app feel like a personal mentor.
**How to apply:** When suggesting features or architecture, consider how they integrate with the existing lesson system and audio-first UX.

## Post-reliability milestone: subscription entitlement redesign

Revisit Inner's complete free/premium entitlement map after Overnight Journey reliability is established. Do not mix this work into the current reliability fixes.

Product principle: free Inner should be a complete practice; paid Inner should be a deeper relationship. Avoid scattered individual locks and present one coherent unlock centered on the full procedural engine, constructed nights, and future personal adaptation.

The free practice should include:
- Chambers 1–4 and the core Garden
- Core dream journal and Morning Return
- Recognition practice and essential Signals
- Ocean Overnight Journey as a permanent, repeatable free experience
- Basic Live Mix
- A constrained Journey Creator
- Guardian 1
- A useful Aeris allowance

Continuing with Inner should include:
- Chambers 5–9, Deeper Garden, and remaining Guardians
- Temple, Forest, Fire, Cosmic, Abyssal Glass, and future procedural Worlds
- Full Live Mix controls and unlimited Remembered Fields
- Full Journey Creator
- Advanced protocol options
- Future journal pattern analysis and personally adaptive protocols
- Expanded Aeris access, without positioning Aeris as the primary subscription reason

The intended subscription message is: explore every world, construct your own journeys, use the full procedural instrument, and eventually let Inner remember and adapt. Reliability and essential recovery or safety behavior must remain available regardless of entitlement.
