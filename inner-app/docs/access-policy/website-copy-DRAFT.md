## Free

- Ocean, Forest, and Quiet Overnight environments.
- Factory Lucid Journeys, including their premium-environment stages.
- Dream Log, including Morning Return and basic reflection.
- Essential playback and comfort controls.
- Live Mix and the chamber mixer.

## Full Experience

- Temple, Abyssal Glass, Cosmic, and Fire as additional Overnight environments.
- Temple comparison nights in Practice Memory.
- Building and saving your own Lucid Journeys in the Journey Creator.

Full Experience does not change the sounds of Live Mix. It adds Overnight environments.

## Keeping what you made

- Saved journeys stay playable if Full Experience ends.
- Deleting a saved journey is always free.
- Your Dream Log is never locked.

## Plain-language promise

- An active session is never interrupted.
- If Inner cannot verify membership, it says so and lets you retry. It will not tell you that you are not a member when it simply cannot check.
